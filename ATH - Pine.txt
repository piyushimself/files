DEPRECATED — DO NOT USE THIS AS A LIVE OR BACKTESTED STRATEGY.

This file contains the former ATH Watchlist Dashboard v7 indicator. Its monthly calculations depended on the chart timeframe, its weekly exit could miss intra-month weekly closes, displayed stop/target levels were not enforced, and its custom compounded per-leg return/CAGR/drawdown figures were not account-level performance metrics.

Use ATH_Weekly_Confirmed_Strategy.pine instead. That replacement:
- Requires a weekly chart and uses only the previous confirmed monthly candle for monthly signals.
- Evaluates the weekly SMA exit on every confirmed weekly bar.
- Places actual Strategy Tester entries and stop/target exit orders.
- Sizes each entry from strategy equity and caps open legs and deployed capital.
- Leaves account-level performance reporting to TradingView Strategy Tester.

Important limitations: the replacement has not been compiled or backtested in TradingView in this repository workflow. It deliberately enters at the first weekly close after the confirmation month, not retroactively at the monthly trigger price. Validate the script, broker costs, Bar Magnifier coverage and fills in TradingView before any live use.

The previous source remains in Git history for audit purposes.
